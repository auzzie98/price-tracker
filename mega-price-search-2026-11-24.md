# Mega Price Search, 30 Models, Nov 24, 2026

Date checked: Tuesday, November 24, 2026. Note: Nov 24, 2026 is a Tuesday, not a Monday. Earlier briefs said Monday by mistake.

Scope: 30 models, 10 GPUs, 10 CPUs, 10 RAM kits. Vendors checked: Amazon, Newegg, Micro Center, Best Buy, B and H, Walmart, Target, Costco, Dell, HP, Lenovo, GameStop, Adorama, Abt Electronics, plus manufacturer direct stores (MSI US Store, Corsair). Method: public web search and page text fetch only, no live browser. All prices are US dollars, new products only. Only listings sold by the retailer itself, or fulfilled by it, count as retailer prices. Third party marketplace sellers are listed separately and are not counted. No price, stock status, or link in this file was invented. Where a price rests on a secondary tracker or a search crawl rather than the retailer's own page text, that is stated. Where a fact could not be verified, it is marked not verified.

## How to read this file

- Current prices first, then historical prices, per category.
- Verification levels: direct page open on Nov 24 is the strongest. Search crawl or aggregator (Pangoly, MaxMyBuild, pctechvault, pricehistory.app, rtx.live, PGrid) is second, and the source and date are given. Anything marked not verified was not confirmed on a page or in a dated source.
- No readable PCPartPicker, CamelCamelCamel US, or Keepa daily history could be retrieved for any of the 30 exact MPNs in this run. Historical points come from Pangoly, MaxMyBuild, NowInStock, PriceSniff, PGrid, pctechvault, pricehistory.app, RamRadar, and dated retailer and press snapshots. Conflicting tracker points are flagged as conflicts, not resolved.
- Retailers with no verified new, retailer-sold listing found for the exact SKUs: Target, Costco, Dell, HP, Lenovo, GameStop, Adorama, Abt Electronics. That is a search result, not proof the product is absent at those retailers.
